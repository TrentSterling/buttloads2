Surface Nets is informed by Mikola Lysenko's [Smooth Voxel Terrain, part 2](https://0fps.net/2012/07/12/smooth-voxel-terrain-part-2/) and Tront Terrain Lab's MIT-licensed stable-vertex, incremental-face architecture. The remake kernel is implemented independently in `src/mesher.js`.

Motherload and A Game About Digging a Hole were researched as game design references. No code, models, textures, audio or text assets were extracted from those games. All scene geometry, interface, lore and sound here are original procedural work for Buttloads 2.
