Cutter, scoop, lance and resonator use both articulated arms; gravity, axe and sling keep one-handed grips. The free arm balances along the direction of travel instead of swinging forward during every strafe. Existing boots, knees, torso travel and authoritative capsules remain unchanged.

The first carry candidate passed grip checks but failed direct native visual inspection: the rear glove disappeared into the bib. The rejected portable and all 27 candidate captures remain under tools/out/miner-carry-candidate. The correction moves the carried tool forward and adjusts its height and steep aiming offsets. The fixture hides the local HUD and tool to expose the miner.

Local camera/controller, Trystero, weapon assets and earlier merge savings retain their sources.
