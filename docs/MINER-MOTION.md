# Remote miner motion, 2.48.0

Remote miners now plant their boots in world space, lift into the next step and bend their knees to reach support. Travel drives the cycle, including backward movement and strafing. The pelvis yields to the stance, the torso counters the step and the weapon follows that torso before the existing palm/grip solve. Stopping finishes a lifted step and then settles both feet individually. First-person input, authoritative capsules and networking are unchanged from 2.47.2.

`node tools/test.mjs` ends with **COMPLETE 551 system checks passed**. Eleven new checks exercise actual `renderCrew` transforms: sustained foot planting, four directions at four speeds, sole orientation and reach, both strafe boot volumes, stationary reported velocity, stopping, all seven moving/firing weapon grips at three aim pitches, teleport/landing/air/reduced motion, bounded support probes, unchanged asset inventory, 30/60/144 Hz rendering, terrain edits, a real supported Player path and portable world replacement. Frozen 2.47.2 fails the sustained planting check with zero consecutive planted frames.

The [six-slide review](../tools/out/miner-motion-review.html) contains 39 matched native pairs: eight front steps, eight side steps, eight strafe steps, eight stopping samples and all seven equipped weapons. All 78 final native frames were personally inspected. Camera and root traces are exact between the pairs; the pure Player paths remain clear and supported. Maximum recorded palm/grip distance is 10.05 mm. Recorded sample playback is not a live frame-rate test. The fixture hides the local HUD and weapon to expose the remote rig.

The [conservation report](../tools/out/miner-motion-conservation.json) records exact native cached/refreshed draws and submitted triangles in every pair. No meshes, model triangles, materials, textures or lights are added. Only `crew-view.js` changes executable behavior. Miner geometry, equipped weapon construction/material ownership, grip solver, support cache and body/equipment batching remain exact. Animation adds two leg solves and upper-body transforms per miner per frame. Foot placement uses at most three floor probes per target; edited supports refresh at a bounded interval. This is additional CPU work, not a claim of free animation or measured FPS improvement.

Rejected revisions are part of the evidence. The opening stride initially overextended at speed, and ledge targets dropped too far from the capsule. Targets now shorten towards the capsule on steep support changes, with a clamped support approximation. Another native capture showed boots and knee pads occupying the same volume during strafing; [the rejected frame](../tools/out/miner-motion-rejected-strafe/strafe-120.png) is retained. Weighted lane offsets still failed the boot-volume regression. Final fore/aft lanes and sufficient pelvis compression pass both strafe directions and are visible throughout the inspected final sequence. Terrain revisions refresh supports without restarting the cycle.

The character still reads as assembled parts. Shoulder and chest forms repeat, knee caps look disconnected at deep bends, the free arm stays restrained and wide strafe steps can look crouched. Faces and clean clothing need a further art pass. World surfaces remain sparse. This checkpoint accepts better support and travel, not the completed hard art. Quiet-machine timing, physical Firefox mouse feel and co-op over separate networks remain unverified.
