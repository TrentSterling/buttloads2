Inside the hole.

A first-person successor to Trent Sterling’s 2015 BUTTLOADS! Cut your own tunnels, follow veins, sell your finds, and build a better rig. Recover heavy machinery, unlock attachments in new strata, light your routes, and follow the roots into something impossible. Blast charges have a 2.6-second fuse. At 9 m, remote satchels stick to rock and wait for H. At 25 m, bore charges cut a 6 m tunnel along your aim and cost two charges. They scatter ore and kick you back; they do not destroy valuables. Work lights fall if their support is removed.

Pink thunderstone seams hold a choice: expose and recover a crystal with E for a spare charge, or blast it to ignite a chain reaction through the mineral pocket. Drilling alone is safe. Recover a middle crystal to break the chain. F records crystals on your survey; J explains discoveries.

Cinder moths live in cave pockets. Use your drill or mining axe (6, unlocked at 9 m) to fight. Their flare warns of a lunge; dodge or lift away. Placed work lights keep them back. Rest at the surface to restore health. If the yard crew rescues you, lost minerals wait in a recovery cache marked on M; E retrieves them.

The Foreman Below draws its armor from three pressure locks. Expose and break the locks to damage its core. Dodge its marked cutting jet or shelter behind rock; hold Space above ground shocks. Defeating it restores power to Ridge Common and unlocks Z, a reusable 12 m foundry bore with a six-second recharge.
