2.18 / packaged

Lantern leviathan + Nell Wick

Find something ancient.
Bring someone new to town.

A huge skeleton lies buried near 220 metres. Excavate its three study sites, light them with placed lamps, and scan the exposed markings. Recover its ember to bring Nell Wick and her lantern cart to Ridge Common.

Nell's $350 living lenses retrofit both existing and future work lights: reach increases from 16 to 24 metres, with a wider refuge from moths. The skeleton uses terrain support and falls as a whole when undermined. Discovery, movement, purchases and the new resident persist.

Verified: all 260 system checks pass, including 12 fossil checks. A fresh campaign earned its equipment, restored the lower stations, excavated and illuminated the fossil, recovered the ember, met Nell, bought the lenses, reloaded and returned to upgraded placed lamps.

Sling / included

Stonewright workshop + mineral sling

The ore is the ammunition.
You can still sell it afterward.

An old launching frame sits in a lower cave. Uncover its two field coils and wake them with resonance, then recover the sling from its console.

Equip it with 7. Hold on a loose mineral to lift and charge it, then release to throw. A fast impact can break a crawler's shell. The mineral remains a physical object with the same identity and value.

Verified route: a fresh claim earned the sling, reloaded a thrown mineral in flight, damaged a crawler, defeated the furnace and returned to town. The sling shows an amber field and a clearance hint when a held mineral catches on rock. Human aiming and combat feel still need a playthrough.

  1. 01Discover and excavateScanner and map support. The workshop follows its terrain support.
  2. 02Charge both field coilsUses your existing resonator; rock blocks the connection.
  3. 03Lift, aim, releaseActual loose minerals, swept collisions, armor damage and physical cover.
  4. 04Keep your mine intactFlight saves preserve the projectile. Pausing releases a held rock without firing.

Included / 2.18
01

A town above the mine

Ridge Common has roads, shop interiors, neighboring land, a well and a survey office. Mara handles supplies and ore; Otis handles equipment, freight and restored return routes. Walk beyond your yard while keeping excavation inside the claim.

02

A rescue with a lasting payoff

Free Inez's stranded survey bell, power its winch and excavate a route home. She becomes a permanent resident, opens the survey office and sells charts of real untouched seams.

03

More than a solid block

Seeded caves contain loops, side rooms and vertical spaces. Repairable cabinets chart passages. The awakened heart opens four lower strata and five more cave networks while preserving older excavations.

04

Tools that can fight

Drill contact, lance armor damage, axe swings, resonance and magic share the combat system. Cinder moths avoid placed lights. Shale crawlers use supported paths, telegraph charges and fall when undermined.

05

Discoveries change your equipment

Restored stations improve deep cutting, lift and scanning. A crawler tooth unlocks Otis's impact axe. Recoveries also unlock freight, return travel, magical tools and optional puzzle rewards.

06

Something waiting at the bottom

The Foreman Below has buried pressure locks, a protected core, a cutting jet and ground shocks. Defeating it unlocks a reusable foundry bore and restores power to the common's well and road.

What has actually been checked.

The new 2.18 journey completes the upper campaign and lower stations, then studies the leviathan using excavation, placed lights and aimed scans. It recovers the ember, buys Nell's lenses through her real counter, reloads and returns to upgraded underground lamps. Focused checks include support removal, old saves, payment, supplies and rock blocking moth protection.

The 2.17 scripted journey started with a fresh claim, earned equipment, recovered both machines, opened the seal, awakened the heart and collected all three geodes. It then earned and bought Eastcut, mined new ore, reached a cave, placed a light and anchor, shipped a haul, sold it and returned below. Both the equipment and shipment survived reloads.

The new parcel checks cover purchase eligibility, payment, interrupted construction, terrain joins, old-save preservation, connected caves, chart coverage, freight, cargo recovery and surface throws. The previous sling, crawler, rescue and furnace systems remain in the regression suite.

What these results do not prove

The renderer and DOM are inert. These checks do not certify WebGL appearance, browser layout, audio playback, combat feel or human pacing. The pilot knows coordinates; simulated time is not a human completion estimate.

Save compatibility and test method

Migration checks retain terrain and mineral IDs. Throws move existing uncollected ore; they do not create inventory copies. Tests use Node and production systems. No OS mouse or keyboard automation is used.
